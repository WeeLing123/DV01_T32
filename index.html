Policy Takeaway (Q1)

Despite policy adjustments in Victoria and New South Wales in 2012 and 2017, national injury counts showed no decline, continuing a steady upward trajectory.

A noticeable drop occurred in 2020 due to pandemic mobility restrictions, followed by a rebound in 2021.

Policy Takeaway (Q4)

First Nations traffic injury hospitalisations are heavily concentrated among young adults aged 17 to 25.

Interventions must account for regional road conditions and culturally tailored youth driver safety programs.
